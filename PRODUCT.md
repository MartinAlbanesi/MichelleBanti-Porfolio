# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The creative and production team of a streaming channel (LUZU is the reference), and others hiring audiovisual producers. They review Michelle Banti in a few minutes, often on a phone, and want to know who she is, what she has produced and how to reach her. Contact is by email or WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

A single-page Spanish portfolio that presents Michelle Banti as an aspiring audiovisual producer for streaming channels, with experience as a content creator and digital journalist. Success is that the right team contacts her. It lives alongside her UGC portfolio on Canva and never mixes with it.

## Positioning

Her real path told by her in first person: MercadoLibre, ETER, TENT, the theatre production and BANCA, with LUZU named openly as her inspiration. It shows a person with a point of view, not a template.

## Operating Context

Static site, zero cost, hosted on Cloudflare Pages (25 MiB per file limit). No backend or form: contact via `mailto:` and WhatsApp. Long videos are hosted on YouTube (unlisted) and embedded lazily; short clips are self-hosted. Michelle edits the copy herself, so all text lives in `src/data/content.ts`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Spanish only.
- One long page with a top index; alternating media + text blocks, one media per block, never several side by side.
- ETER is shown as a curated slider (videos, audio, images) plus a link to the full work (link pending).
- Zero-cost hosting; mobile first; 3D only as a subtle detail (clapperboard), planned for a later milestone.
- Palette: dark theme in deep burgundies (Charred Brown, Saving Light) with Tostado, Red Gravel, Rosa arena and Sweet Cream (see DESIGN.md). No green, pink, violet, light blue or pastel colors.
- Undecided: domain, the ETER full-work link, the TENT clip, photos for the "Hacia dónde voy" steps (mostly landscape).

## Brand Commitments

Name: Michelle Banti. Voice: close, personal and creative, first person. Personality: strong, persevering, outspoken, motivating, persuasive, sociable, independent, a fast learner who likes to teach from experience. Contact: michelleba.community@gmail.com and WhatsApp 11 6563-2086.

## Evidence on Hand

Photos (`src/assets/hero-selfie.jpeg`, `sobre-mi-selfie.jpeg`, two 2023 photos with a camera, project not confirmed), three ETER videos (`Streaming producción`, `streaming por.si.o.por.no`, `Adolescencia Cobertura`, pending YouTube upload), a moodboard PDF (project not confirmed). Now on the site: BANCA, theatre, MercadoLibre, ETER and "Quién soy" photos. Absent, and not to be invented: the TENT clip, metrics, testimonials and client names.

## Product Principles

1. Show real experience; never invent metrics, quotes or credits.
2. One medium per block, always with its text, so every piece of work is understood.
3. Phone first: the reviewer is probably on a phone.
4. Her personality comes before any template.
5. Keep it light and free: no backend, no heavy media in the page.
